The Application collects certain information when you download and use it. This information may include:

- **Subscription Purchase Records / 訂閱購買紀錄:** When you subscribe to Quotify Pro (monthly, yearly, or lifetime), your purchase information is collected and processed through RevenueCat, our subscription management service. / 當您訂閱 Quotify Pro（月付、年付或終身方案）時，您的購買資訊會透過我們的訂閱管理服務 RevenueCat 進行收集和處理。

該應用程式不會收集您行動裝置的精確位置資訊。
